I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in the Department of Environmental Science, Policy and Management at UC Berkeley, where I am co-advised by Dr. Sunaura Taylor and Dr. Rachel Morello-Frosch. I also hold an MPH in epidemiology and biostatistics from UC Berkeley and a BA in Environmental Biology from Barnard College.

My research examines the impact of climate change on disabled people, with a particular focus on wildfire smoke exposure, extreme heat exposure, and Public Safety Power Shutoffs in California. I approach this research through the lenses of critical disability studies and environmental justice, insisting that disabled people are not just medically vulnerable, but are made vulnerable through specific policy decisions that fail to consider the needs of disabled people in the face of disasters.

I identify as disabled and am an adult living with cystic fibrosis. I am committed to conducting applied research that informs environmental health policy and advances health equity in the face of climate change, as well as advancing disabled representation in academia.
